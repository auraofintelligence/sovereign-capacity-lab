A Fair Go in the Age of Superintelligence

Joyful responsible abundance needs useful machines, reliable clean power and people who retain authority over both.

This laboratory connects Luke Nathan Hayes's mining and materials paper with Joyful Responsible Abundance and Extreme Matter Atlas. It gives the proposed missing middle a physical account: what to build, what it contains, how to power it and how to keep it working.

The global manufacturing scenario and an Australian civic rollout are separate scales. Start with the power budget or change the year below. Then examine the material, mineral, repair and infrastructure requirements.

Default scenario dated 7 November 2026.

  1. 01
    Power and the clean energy budget

    Build new capability while preserving the electricity allocated to clean-energy transition and essential development.

  2. 02
    Machines materials and long service lives

    Explore annual production, surviving fleets, refurbishment, motors and the material in each machine.

  3. 03
    Generation storage and substitute chemistries

    Separate power ratings, delivered energy, losses and the atomic materials needed by a chosen design.

  4. 04
    From atomic elements to industrial supply

    Substitute a source mineral, change its grade and recovery, then compare the required output with existing industry.

  5. 05
    Put the whole project in the material ledger

    Create a bill of materials for factories, grids, robots, panels, service parts and other equipment.

  6. 06
    Orbital power mass and transport

    Size on-board generation, heat rejection, payload transport and power delivered to other users.

  7. 07
    Australian capability before and beyond 2032

    Connect population growth, domestic industry, civic robots, sovereign compute and global exports.

Where these projects meet

  • Joyful Responsible Abundance sets out the social purpose, capacity and ownership arrangements.
  • Extreme Matter Atlas explores elements, crystal structures, metamaterials and material discovery. Its atomic dataset powers the formula calculations here.
  • A Fair Go in the Age of Superintelligence and the Australian installation plan connect postcode-level compute with personal, local and national sovereignty.
  • GAJRA Earth, the Global Association for Joyful Responsible Abundance on Earth, extends the missing middle through distributed capability around the world.
  • A Protopian Gambit, including We Go Beyond and Joyful Responsible Abundance, expresses the cultural purpose of building a world people want to keep participating in.
