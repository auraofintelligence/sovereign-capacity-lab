A practical deadline and an ongoing legacy

The Brisbane 2032 Olympic Games run from 23 July to 8 August, followed by the Paralympic Games from 24 August to 5 September. Queensland Government dates, checked 7 November 2026.

Hayes proposes using that horizon to develop enduring community capability: trained crews, local computing, civic robots, repair workshops, reliable power and public learning spaces. These calculators size a proposed civic programme. Games operations and official procurement require their own published equipment and load schedules.

The physical missing middle consists of locally governed infrastructure. Individual Auras are software that can use it. Civic robots support useful work and resilience under distributed authority. A single organisation does not command the whole network.

Where these projects meet

  • Joyful Responsible Abundance sets out the social purpose, capacity and ownership arrangements.
  • Extreme Matter Atlas explores elements, crystal structures, metamaterials and material discovery. Its atomic dataset powers the formula calculations here.
  • A Fair Go in the Age of Superintelligence and the Australian installation plan connect postcode-level compute with personal, local and national sovereignty.
  • GAJRA Earth, the Global Association for Joyful Responsible Abundance on Earth, extends the missing middle through distributed capability around the world.
  • A Protopian Gambit, including We Go Beyond and Joyful Responsible Abundance, expresses the cultural purpose of building a world people want to keep participating in.
